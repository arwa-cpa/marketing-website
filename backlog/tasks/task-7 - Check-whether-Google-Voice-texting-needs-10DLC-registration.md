## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
The site publishes the Google Voice number as text-able. Check whether business texting needs 10DLC registration.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Final Summary

<!-- SECTION:FINAL_SUMMARY:BEGIN -->
No 10DLC registration needed. 10DLC covers application-to-person (A2P) texting: automated, bulk or software-sent messages. Arwa uses Google Voice person-to-person only: visitors text her and she replies by hand, one to one, with no automation or promotions. Google Voice offers no 10DLC registration anyway; its Acceptable Use Policy and the Workspace terms simply forbid automated and bulk commercial texting.

If texting ever becomes automated or bulk (e.g. appointment reminders sent to many clients), it would need a provider that supports A2P 10DLC registration instead of Google Voice.
<!-- SECTION:FINAL_SUMMARY:END -->
