Taxes can be complicated. Let’s make it easy.

Tax regulations change often, and living, working or owning a business across the U.S. and Canada adds filing obligations that shift as your career, business and family evolve. It’s easy to feel like you’re missing something — or paying more than you should.

We break it down so you can understand it, and make confident decisions from there.

Services

Cross-border tax is the focus, alongside tax preparation and accounting for individuals, families, S corps, C corps, solopreneurs and freelancers.

U.S.–Canada cross-border tax

Our primary specialty. For individuals and business owners living, working, or moving between the U.S. and Canada — we handle dual filings, treaty positions, and the reporting both countries require.

Business tax preparation

Returns and planning for S corps, C corps, and growing businesses, so filings stay accurate and on time every year.

Individual tax preparation

Personal returns handled with care and explained clearly, for individuals and families with straightforward or more complex situations.

Small business accounting

Bookkeeping and accounting support for solopreneurs and freelancers, so your books stay in order year-round, not just at tax time.
